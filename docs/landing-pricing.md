## Pricing (NGN + USD)

| Plan | NGN/mo | ≈ USD/mo | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | ₦15,000 | $10 | 1 clinic, EHR, appointments, billing, email support |
| Pro | ₦40,000 | $26 | + pharmacy, lab, wards, SMS reminders, analytics |
| Enterprise | ₦100,000+ | $65+ | multi-branch, NHIA integration, custom workflows, AI |
| Custom | Tailored | — | government / large hospitals, on-premise, 24/7 support |

Every plan includes: hospital website (`yourhospital.skycare.app` + custom domain) and the patient PWA app.
